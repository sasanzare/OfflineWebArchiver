## Preservation conclusions

- No selected source lost a confirmed requirement, architecture boundary, security condition, recovery invariant, or evidence identity.
- Legacy aggregate status values were not copied into official `status`; they were separated into the approved `owa` fields or retained as explicitly historical prose.
- The only merge is the documented Phase 3 architecture duplicate. It preserves both source paths and adds the unique architecture context once.
- The only intentional omissions are navigation-only or volatile next-phase statements and content mapped to later Phase 5 Concepts. These omissions are recorded above and do not remove source files.
- No source conflict was silently resolved. The product next-phase wording conflict is explicitly deferred to the product planning authority and Phase 5.
