platform facts. A recommendation is not a final outcome. Resolution requires the
named evidence, owner approval, an ADR for architectural decisions, and updates
to scope, acceptance, risks, and phase plans where affected.

## Resolution workflow

1. Owner attaches evidence and proposed outcome.
2. Architecture/security decisions receive review and an ADR under
   `docs/project/adr/`.
3. Product Owner confirms affected requirements, acceptance criteria, risks,
   fixtures, and phases are updated.
4. The row keeps its historical question/recommendation, records the final
   outcome and ADR, and changes blocking status.

No open item authorizes later-phase implementation before its deadline.

Detailed OKF-specific questions are maintained in
[`okf-bootstrap/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](../../okf-bootstrap/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) as
`OKF-OD-001..025`. They do not replace or renumber this project decision register.
