## Scoring method

- **Probability (P):** 1 rare (<10%), 2 unlikely (10–29%), 3 possible (30–59%),
  4 likely (60–79%), 5 almost certain (>=80%).
- **Impact (I):** 1 negligible, 2 minor, 3 material phase/quality impact, 4 major
  release/security/target impact, 5 severe legal, security, data-loss, or
  release-blocking impact.
- **Score:** `P × I`.
- **Severity:** low 1–4; medium 5–9; high 10–15; critical 16–25.

Probability and impact are reassessed at each phase gate. High/critical risks
require a named owner, active mitigation task, warning signal, and contingency.
