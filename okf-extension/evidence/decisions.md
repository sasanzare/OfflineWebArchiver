# Decision Evidence

This authored extension guide explains decision evidence. Accepted ADRs and open-decision records are indexed through the decision and evidence registries.

The authoritative narrative remains the relevant ADR or open-decision source; the registries provide identifiers and traceability.

Product Phase 11 is recorded by `docs/project/adr/ADR-050-secret-store-and-sensitive-data-protection.md`.
It accepts the project-scoped Secret Store, Portable Vault key hierarchy,
metadata-only transport boundary, explicit Secure Export, diagnostic allowlist,
and centralized redaction. It does not close the pre-existing Phase 9/10 gate.
